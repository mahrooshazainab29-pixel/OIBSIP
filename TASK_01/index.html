How Mahroo unfolds

Each bottle is layered in three stages so the scent shifts naturally across the day.

Top Notes

Bright Opening

Crisp bitter orange and gentle pink pepper awaken the first spray with a clean, lively lift.

Heart Notes

Velvet Floral Core

Lush Damask rose petals and green geranium unfold at the center—floral, calm, and never overly sweet.

Base Notes

Warm Botanical Trail

Creamy sandalwood, smoked oud, and golden myrrh linger close to the skin well into the evening.

Crafted personally by Mahroo

Every batch is blended and rested in small glass vessels by Mahroo, letting natural botanical oils harmonize before bottling.

  • Small-batch maceration: Aged for six weeks for round, smooth edges.
  • Botanical focus: Harvested flower, resin, citrus, and wood extracts.
  • Refillable glass: Heavy flacons designed to be kept and replenished.
